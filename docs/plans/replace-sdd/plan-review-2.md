## Unnamed risks
- The `flow` plugin is installed globally, so stories 03 and 05 reach proyecto_san_sebastian on the next `ai-update`. That project has a live lead plan due 2026-10-29 that depends on `next` and `tasks.md`. Likelihood: medium. Mitigation: keep the `tasks.md` fallback (finding 6), or release stories 03 and 05 after 2026-10-29.
- The "commit bookkeeping before any stop" step could sweep unrelated untracked files into a commit (the repo has untracked `docs/ideas.md` and `docs/plans/*` right now). Likelihood: medium. Mitigation: pair stages only named paths and never uses `git add -A` or `.`.
- Structural tests for skills mostly detect changes ("grep heading"). Red-to-green passes almost trivially, and the real behaviour check (evals) costs quota. Likelihood: medium. Mitigation: assert only the contract strings other skills parse, and require the eval result at Done (finding 12).
- In other projects, a pre-commit hook or branch CI that runs tests would reject the red `test:` commit, and the agent might then reach for `--no-verify`. Likelihood: low. Mitigation: pair stops with "hook rejected red commit" and never bypasses hooks.
- A stale red marker turns off the harness in every later session (finding 1). Likelihood: medium. Mitigation: bind the marker to the branch and sha, and clean it at pair start.

## Questions for the user (max 5)
1. Should `backlog` and `story` be model-invocable, with only `pair` slash-only, or should all three be slash-only like hola-ai? Why it matters: it decides the eval design and whether the `CLAUDE.md` Workflow line can route to them. My recommendation: `backlog` and `story` model-invocable with narrow triggers; `pair` slash-only.
2. Should the new loop skills reach OpenCode and Antigravity in v1 (the installer links them today), or be held back until `multi-agent-port`? Why it matters: `pair` breaks there and the generic names can collide. My recommendation: exclude them from `link_skills` until the port.
3. Who merges a finished story branch into `main`, and how? Why it matters: the `[x] <sha>` record and the test-first evidence only survive a no-squash, no-rebase merge. My recommendation: pair offers `git merge --no-ff` after your OK.
4. Can `flow` changes (stories 03 and 05) reach proyecto_san_sebastian before its 2026-10-29 deadline? Why it matters: `next` and the legacy plan path change under a live plan. My recommendation: ship with the `tasks.md` fallback, or hold the `flow` update until after 2026-10-29.
