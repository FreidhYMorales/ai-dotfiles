# Plan review: clean-install
Verdict: FAIL
Summary: The chain and its order are sound, and the research holds up against the code. The plan fails on one blocker: the inventory is an allowlist that leaves out at least 15 GB of unique files on the partition being formatted, and nothing protects against a failed reinstall. There are also major gaps in first-boot network, fallback, publishing and estimates. All have small fixes, and no MUST misses a deadline because there is none.

All source files were read, and live state was checked with read-only commands. No secret file was opened.

## Unnamed risks
- Rolling-release drift: a green VM pass goes stale before the format (`quickshell-git` and `yay` packages are unpinned; `pacman -Sy` at bootstrap.sh:55 is a partial upgrade) — medium — Re-run the pass if it is more than about 7 days old. Back up the current AUR build of quickshell. Record verified versions.
- External disk capacity and filesystem: the unique data, plus models (29 GB), Music (34 GB) and optional VMs and Steam (114 GB), may not fit, and the filesystem may not keep permissions — medium — Measure first. Use ext4 or LUKS and tar with permissions. Keep a second copy of irreplaceable coursework and keys.
- Password manager or 2FA locked behind the wiped browser profiles or keyring (`.config/zen`, `.local/share/keyrings`) — medium — Log in from a second device before the format.
- No working agent or docs during the format, since Claude and the local plan are gone — medium — Keep the checklist on a phone or on paper, and confirm the docs can be read from a second device.
- Hard-coded `/home/deadlock` and `DOTFILES IA` paths make a VM with another user or path fail falsely, or pass for the wrong reason (`.gitconfig`, `fastfetch`) — low — Use identical user and path, and treat other values as unsupported.
- Publishing commits personal data that cannot be recalled, because forks and caches outlive a later rewrite (noted at auditorias/optimizacion/README.md:35) — low to medium — See question 2.
- Manually installed tools lost: 243 explicit packages today, including libvirt, KiCad, Arduino, DBeaver, AnyDesk and scanners — high likelihood, low damage — Save `pacman -Qqe` and decide which ones join bootstrap, without blocking the format.

## Questions for the user (max 5)
1. What happens to the disk: keep p7 `Files`, remove p2-p4 (Debian, Fedora, openSUSE), keep hibernation swap, and encrypt with LUKS? — The answer drives backup size, paths, lid behaviour and the rollback option, and encryption is cheapest at format time. — Recommendation: keep p7 unformatted but still back up its irreplaceable data. Remove p2-p4 only after confirming they hold nothing you need. Keep swap of at least RAM size. Decide LUKS now.
2. Do you want the three repos public, or is signing in first acceptable? — Publishing exists only so that clones need no auth, and it cannot be fully undone. Installing `github-cli` and running `gh auth login` before the clone would remove that exposure. — Recommendation: if you have no other reason to publish, stay private and sign in first. This is cheaper and safer, but `curl | bash` then needs a token or a local clone.
3. Must polish block the format: stories 15, 33, 24, 29 and the parity views? — This is the largest and only unbounded item. — Recommendation: no. Reinstall once 19, 27 and 28 are done, and ship the rest later through a submodule bump. Tell me which parity views you actually use.
4. Which omitted data do you want to keep: VMs in libvirt (52 GB), Steam (62 GB), Bottles, Docker, browser profiles, Downloads, session history? — The denylist needs your decision, and I cannot judge its value. — Recommendation: keep everything by default on the image, exclude only what you name, and keep Documents, browser profiles and the Windows Server VM.
5. Which password manager holds the restic password, and can you open it and pass 2FA from a device other than this laptop? — It is the single dependency that recovery cannot replace. — Recommendation: prove this from another device in stage 0, before anything else proceeds.
